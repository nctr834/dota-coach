# Dota 2 Item Guide - RAG Context

## Major Items (Alphabetical)

**Abyssal Blade** - 6250g, builds from Basher + Sange
Provides: +35 damage, +26 str,
25% bash chance (melee only),
1.2s bash, active 1.6s stun that pierces spell immunity
Buy when: you need BKB-piercing lockdown, you're a melee core, enemy has slippery heroes (Storm, Ember, AM, Void Spirit), you need to cancel TPs through BKB
Skip when: you're ranged (10% bash is awful), you need damage more than control, enemy team isn't mobile, you can't close the gap to use the active

**Aeon Disk** - 3000g, builds from Energy Booster + Vitality Booster
Provides: +250 HP, +300 mana, passive that triggers at 70% HP dealing no damage for 2.5s with 75% status resist,
105-165s CD based on hero level
Buy when: you're a squishy support getting jumped, enemy has instant burst (Tiny combo, PA crit), you keep dying before using spells, you're Medusa/Storm and need to survive initiation
Skip when: enemy has DoT-heavy damage (Veno, Jakiro) that triggers it early, you need offensive items instead, game is going late and CD becomes too long

**Aether Lens** - 2275g, builds from Energy Booster + Void Stone
Provides: +300 mana, +2.5 mana regen, +225 cast range
Buy when: you're a position 4/5 caster who needs range to stay alive, you're building toward Octarine, you play Pudge/Mirana/Bane and range matters a lot
Skip when: you're a core who doesn't benefit from range, you need survivability more than range, your hero has huge cast range already

**Arcane Blink** - 6800g, builds from Blink Dagger + Mystic Staff
Provides: +25 int,
1400 blink range (vs 1200 normal), instant 250 HP and 100 mana restore on arrival
Buy when: you're an int caster who already wants Blink (Puck, QoP, Sand King), you have mana-hungry combos, the extra 200 range matters for initiations
Skip when: you need the gold elsewhere, you don't care about the int (str/agi heroes), base Blink is sufficient for your purposes

**Armlet of Mordiggian** - 2500g, builds from Helm of Iron Will + Gloves of Haste + Blades of Attack
Provides: +6 armor, +25 AS, +15 damage, +5 HP regen, toggle for +35 damage +25 str +4 armor (drains 45 HP/s)
Buy when: you're a melee str carry (CK, WK, Huskar, Lifestealer), you can toggle well, you have lifesteal or regen to sustain it
Skip when: you're bad at toggling, you're ranged, you don't have sustain, enemy has DoTs that make toggling dangerous (Venomancer)

**Assault Cuirass** - 5125g, builds from Hyperstone + Platemail + Recipe
Provides: +10 armor, +30 AS, aura +30 AS and +5 armor to allies,
-5 armor to enemies
Buy when: your team lacks armor, you're against heavy physical damage, you're a carry or offlaner who can build it, someone needs to get it and you're the best candidate
Skip when: someone else is building it (doesn't stack), you desperately need other stats, enemy is mostly magic damage

**Battle Fury** - 3900g, builds from Perseverance + Claymore + Broadsword
Provides: +50 damage, +7.5 HP regen, +2.75 mana regen,
60% cleave
Buy when: you're a melee carry who wants to flash farm (AM, Jugg, PA), enemy has illusion heroes (PL, Naga), you want to accelerate into late game
Skip when: you're ranged, you need to fight early, you're behind and need immediate impact, you're a hero who farms with spells anyway

**Black King Bar** - 4050g, builds from Ogre Axe + Mithril Hammer + Recipe
Provides: +24 damage, +10 str, active spell immunity (9/8/7/6s based on uses)
Buy when: you're a core who needs to hit people without being disabled, enemy has tons of disables or magic damage, you're getting kited or bursted in fights
Skip when: enemy damage is mostly physical and right-clicks, your hero doesn't care about spells (Lifestealer has built-in), you can avoid spells with mobility or you're a pos 5 who won't get this much gold

**Blade Mail** - 2300g, builds from Broadsword + Chainmail + Robe of the Magi
Provides: +6 armor, +15 damage, passive 15% damage return, active 85% damage return for 5.5s
Buy when: you're getting focused, you're a tanky initiator (Axe, Legion, Centaur), enemy has high-damage heroes who will kill themselves (PA, Ursa)
Skip when: enemy can disengage easily, enemy has lifesteal to heal through it, you're too squishy to survive the initial burst anyway

**Blink Dagger** - 2250g, no components (basic item)
Provides: 1200 range instant teleport (960 if you overshoot),
3s CD on taking damage
Buy when: you need initiation (Axe, Tidehunter, Enigma, Sand King), you need escape as a support, positioning wins fights for your hero
Skip when: you have built-in mobility that's sufficient, you're a right-clicker who needs damage items, enemy has lots of DoTs that disable it

**Bloodstone** - 4350g, builds from Soul Booster + Voodoo Mask
Provides: +450 HP, +450 mana, +3 mana regen,
25% spell lifesteal, active 3x spell lifesteal for 5s
Buy when: you're a spell-spamming core (Storm, Leshrac, Timbersaw), you need sustain in fights through spell lifesteal
Skip when: you don't spam spells enough to benefit, you need defensive items, you can't survive to cast multiple spells

**Bloodthorn** - 6625g, builds from Orchid + Crystalys + Recipe
Provides: +10 damage, +95 AS, +10 int, +6.5 HP regen, +3.25 mana regen,
40% crit chance, active 5s silence that makes all attacks crit for 60% bonus damage and applies 35% spell amp debuff
Buy when: you started Orchid and want to scale it, enemy has key spell-dependent heroes (Storm, Puck), you're Clinkz/QoP/NP and want damage + control
Skip when: enemy has Manta/BKB/Lotus/Euls to dispel it, you need other items more, you can't afford to upgrade your Orchid right now

**Boots of Bearing** - 4225g, builds from Tranquil Boots + Drum of Endurance + Recipe
Provides: +65 MS, +8 str, +8 int, +18 HP regen, active 6s aura giving +15% MS and +50% AS to allies
Buy when: you're a position 4/5 who finished Tranquils + Drums and want to upgrade, your team benefits heavily from the MS/AS boost, you're pushing as 5
Skip when: you're a core (waste of slot), you'd rather have Guardian Greaves, you need defensive items instead

**Butterfly** - 5450g, builds from Eaglesong + Quarterstaff + Talisman of Evasion
Provides: +35 agi, +25 damage, +20% AS, +35% evasion, can activate to lose evasion for +35% MS
Buy when: you're an agi carry who wants damage and survivability (AM, Spec, TB, Morph), enemy has right-clickers without MKB, you want late-game scaling
Skip when: enemy already has MKB or is building it, you need other stats more, enemy is mostly magic damage

**Crimson Guard** - 3725g, builds from Vanguard + Buckler + Recipe

60% chance to block 75/50 damage (melee/ranged), active 7s 100% block for allies
Buy when: enemy has summons or illusions (PL, Brood, NP), enemy has fast-attacking heroes where block is value, you're a frontline tank
Skip when: enemy has few right-clickers, enemy hits slowly with big damage (Sven, Tiny), someone else already has it (active doesn't stack)

**Daedalus** - 5100g, builds from Crystalys + Demon Edge + Recipe

30% chance for 225% crit
Buy when: you already have attack speed and need raw damage, you want to one-shot supports, you're a carry going late (PA, Jugg, WK)

**Disperser** - 6100g, builds from Diffusal Blade + Eaglesong + Recipe

40 mana burn, active self-dispel + phase + 100% MS for 5s OR purge + slow enemy
Buy when: you started Diffusal and need an upgrade, you need a self-dispel on an agi carry, you want mobility without Blink

**Dragon Lance** - 1900g, builds from 2x Ogre Axe + 2x Band of Elvenskin

## Core Items by Role

### Carry staples:
Battle Fury (flash farm), Black King Bar (spell immunity), Manta Style (dispel + illusions), Butterfly (evasion + damage), Satanic (late-game lifesteal), Daedalus (crit damage), Abyssal Blade (BKB-piercing lockdown), Skadi (stats + slow)

### Offlane staples:
Blink Dagger (initiation), Black King Bar, Pipe of Insight (magic protect), Crimson Guard (physical protect), Assault Cuirass (armor aura), Heaven's Halberd (disarm), Lotus Orb (reflect), Shiva's Guard (anti-healing)

### Support staples:
Force Staff (save), Glimmer Cape (magic protection), Eul's (setup + dispel), Aether Lens (cast range), Guardian Greaves (sustain + dispel), Spirit Vessel (anti-healing), Solar Crest (buff/debuff), Blink Dagger (positioning)

### Int core staples:
Bloodstone (spell lifesteal), Octarine Core (CDR), Eul's (setup), Scythe of Vyse (disable), Kaya upgrades (spell amp), Wind Waker (mobility), Ethereal Blade (burst)
